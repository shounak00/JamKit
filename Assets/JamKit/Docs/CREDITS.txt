Placeholder art and sound shipped in this version
-------------------------------------------------
All sprites in Assets/JamKit/Art/2D (spr_*.png) and all sounds in Assets/JamKit/Audio (sfx_*.wav)
are generated procedurally by JamKit's own builder script (JamKitBuilder). They are released
under CC0 1.0 (public domain): use them anywhere, no credit required.

Mixamo
------
No Mixamo files are included in this kit. If your game uses a Mixamo character or animation,
credit "Character/animations from Mixamo (Adobe)" on your game page.
Never redistribute the raw Mixamo FBX files.

Using JamKit in a jam game
--------------------------
You may ship games made with JamKit freely. Please disclose it as a pre-made asset, e.g.:
  "Made with JamKit by Shounak Sobahani (MIT) — github.com/shounak00/JamKit"
